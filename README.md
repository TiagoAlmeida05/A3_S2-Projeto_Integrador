# jUPiter QDA 

**jUPiter QDA** is a high-performance qualitative research environment. 

Designed to streamline document management, audio transcription, and complex qualitative coding, jUPiter QDA provides a professional-grade workspace for researchers to analyze data, build insights, and manage their research projects with precision.

## Core Features

*   **Desktop-Native Experience:** A dedicated, distraction-free environment that runs in its own native window.
*   **Advanced Qualitative Coding:**
    *   **Hierarchical Codebooks:** Manage complex research themes with flexible parent-child relationships.
    *   **Quick-Code Interface:** Highlight any text to apply codes instantly.
    *   **Visual Margin Sidebar:** Track your coding progress and segment density in real-time.
*   **Integrated Transcription:** Efficiently process audio interviews with local transcription tools.
*   **Data Portability:** Research without lock-in. Export your work at any time in industry-standard formats:
    *   **REFI-QDA (.qdpx):** For cross-software compatibility.
    *   **Excel/CSV:** For statistical analysis and reporting.
*   **Search & Jump:** Power-search across all your documents with instant navigation to results for fast, efficient analysis.

## Getting Started

### Installation
1. Download the latest release of **jUPiter QDA** for your operating system from the **[Releases Page]**.
2. **Windows:** Run the `jUPiter.QDA.Setup.1.0.0.exe ` installer.
3. **Linux:** Install the `jUPiter.QDA.Setup.AppImage` and run it.

### Launching the App
Simply launch **jUPiter QDA** from your desktop or applications menu. The application will initialize in its own native window, ready for you to create or open a project.

## Workflow Highlights

*   **Projects:** Create a new project and define your local storage path. jUPiter QDA manages your project assets and metadata within your workspace.
*   **Importing:** Simply drag and drop documents and audio files into the interface. 
*   **Coding:**
    *   Highlight text to trigger the Quick-Code menu.
    *   Use the **Search Bar** to find keywords across your entire dataset.
    *   Clicking search results automatically loads the document and centers the context for you.
*   **Safety First:** Use `Ctrl + Z` (or `Cmd + Z`) to undo your last coding or deletion action at any time.

## Reliability
jUPiter QDA is designed for serious research. We prioritize performance, data structure, and reliability, allowing you to focus on your insights while the application handles the heavy lifting of document organization and data cataloging.

## Technical Requirements
*   **OS:** Windows 10/11 or modern Linux distributions.
*   **RAM:** 8GB+ recommended for large projects and efficient processing.
